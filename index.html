Quick answers

How do I throw? Select a target on the ice, confirm it, then swipe forward from the stone. Swipe speed controls weight; a curved swipe adds spin.

How do I sweep? Use the on-screen sweep controls while a stone is moving.

Can I resume a match? Use Save & Exit during a match, then choose Continue from the home screen.
