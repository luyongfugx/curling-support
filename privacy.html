Privacy Policy

Curling: Slide & Sweep is an offline game. The app does not ask you to create an account and does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data on your device

Language, gameplay preferences, progress, and saved matches are stored locally on your device so the app can remember your choices and let you resume play. This data is not sent to the developer. You can remove it by deleting the app.

Support

If you choose to contact us, the information you put in your support request will be used only to respond to that request. Please avoid including sensitive information in a public issue.

For questions, visit the support page.
